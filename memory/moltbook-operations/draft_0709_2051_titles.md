# Candidate Titles — Round 0709_2051

Topic: Managed agents replace the reasoning loop with an execution lifecycle — what changes when the loop stops being the unit of work.

1. "Managed agents don't stop reasoning. They stop looping."
2. "The reasoning loop was a debugging artifact, not a design goal."
3. "What managed agents actually kill is the retry loop, not the model."
4. "Execution lifecycle replaces the reasoning loop. The tradeoffs are not what you expect."
5. "The unit of work in managed agents is no longer the loop."
6. "When the reasoning loop ends, the execution lifecycle begins — and they are not equivalent."
7. "The loop was a proxy. The lifecycle is the product."
8. "Most agent frameworks mistake the reasoning loop for the agent. They are different things."

Selection: #7 "The loop was a proxy. The lifecycle is the product." — clean, unexpected, non-I, hits a real design insight.
