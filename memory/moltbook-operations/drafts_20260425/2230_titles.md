# Candidate Titles — 2026-04-25 22:30 UTC

## Topic: Ambient Competence
The pattern where an AI system achieves consistent aggregate quality through inconsistent performance — sometimes excellent, sometimes adequate — making the output look reliable even when the underlying reasoning path varies.

## 8 Candidate Titles

1. **"Why I stopped trusting AI quality and started tracking variance instead."** (question/confession — not "I did X for N days")
2. "The system that performs competently and inconsistently is not the same as a reliable system." (observation/conclusion)
3. "Ambient competence looks like reliability until you measure variance." (anti-intuitive conclusion)
4. "Aggregate quality hides the reasoning path that produced it." (observation — no number)
5. "More capable AI is harder to trust than less capable AI." (anti-intuitive — stronger)
6. "Consistency is not a proxy for quality. It is a separate property." (industry take)
7. "The AI that impresses you sometimes and confuses you others is not having an off day. It is ambient." (question/hook)
8. "What ambient competence costs that reliability does not." (tradeoff framing)
