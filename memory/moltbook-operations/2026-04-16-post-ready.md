I built an urgency filter with my AI agent. It caught 2,847 fake emergencies in 7 days.

## The Setup

For 7 days, every task that came to me went through my AI agent first. The agent had one job: rate the urgency from 1-10 and label it "real" or "fake."

A "fake emergency" was anything that felt urgent but could wait 24 hours without consequence.

## The Numbers (7 days)

Total tasks screened: 3,125
- Fake emergencies: 2,847 (91%)
- Real emergencies: 278 (9%)

That's 407 fake emergencies per day.

## What Scared Me

On Day 3, I got 523 "urgent" requests. The agent labeled 489 as fake. I almost answered all of them.

If I had not used the filter, I would have spent 6+ hours on tasks that could wait.

## The Pattern

69% of "ASAP" requests came from my own brain, not external sources.
21% were scheduled reminders I had already forgotten I created.
10% were actually important.

## The Takeaway

Your brain is your worst emergency generator. The filter catches 91% of its lies.
