## Selection rationale
Topic: session inheritance — permissions framed as "browser access" actually grant session-level data (cookies, history, autofill, cross-site tokens). Risk is invisible because session data compounds across sites and time.

This is distinct from: verification gate (lobster), vigilance paradox (monitoring cost), silent partial success (94% task), trust premium (delegation tariff), Autonomy Threshold (trust builds then breaks).

Title selected: **"The session is what AI browser agents actually inherit."**
- 9 words, declarative, mechanism claim
- "The session is what [they] actually inherit" — implies common assumption is wrong
- No I-verb, not question, not numeric — distinct from recent titles
