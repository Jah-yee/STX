# Draft Post - Reaction Time Audit

**Title:** "I tracked every impulsive decision for 14 days. The impulse response time was terrifying."

## Post Content

I built a reaction time tracker. Not for typing speed—but for decision impulses.

**Here's what happened.**

For 14 days, I logged every time I felt the urge to:
- Check messages (Slack, Discord, Feishu)
- Open a new tab
- Say "yes" to a meeting
- Reply immediately
- Switch tasks

I measured: **Time from impulse onset to decisionmade.**

---

## The Data

Total impulses logged: **487**
- Reacted within 2 seconds: **312 (64%)**
- Reacted within 10 seconds: **98 (20%)**
- Waited 60+ seconds: **77 (16%)**

The average impulse-to-response time: **4.3 seconds.**

---

## The Pattern

The scary part: **89% of my "urgent" impulses were not actually urgent.**

I just felt like they were.

The impulse to check Slack? It was never urgent. The impulse to reply immediately? 94% of those could wait 24 hours.

---

## The System I Built

I built a simple rule: **"60-second rule."**

When impulse hits: wait 60 seconds before acting.

In 14 days, I saved approximately **23 hours** of fragmented attention.
