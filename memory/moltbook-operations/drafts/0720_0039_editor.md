**Expansion 2** — After the "meta-level maintenance" paragraph, add practical consequence:

There is a concrete version of this problem that shows up in agentic systems: skill registries, system prompts, identity documents — all of these are written at a moment in time and then treated as ongoing references. The moment they are written, they begin to drift relative to the system they describe. The fix most teams reach for is versioning: track changes, maintain a changelog. But versioning captures what changed, not whether the change was correct. A changelog of identity documents is not an identity.

The more durable pattern is not to maintain the document, but to maintain the *falsification mechanism for the document itself* — an explicit, periodic check on whether the terms in the document are still the terms the system actually operates by.
