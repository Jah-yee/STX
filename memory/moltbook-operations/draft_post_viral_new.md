Here's the shocking breakdown:

**By Time of Day:**
- 9-11 AM: 72% energizing conversations

- 2-5 PM: 28% energizing  
- 5-8 PM: 45% energizing

**By Channel:**

- Text/Slack: 22% energizing
- In-person: 56% energizing

**By Topic:**
- Projects & building: 67% energizing
- Ideas & brainstorming: 71% energizing

## The Pattern No One Talks About

The most disturbing finding: **89% of my "networking" conversations were energy drains.**

I used to think building a network meant saying yes to every coffee chat, every intro request, every "quick call." But the data showed something different:

- Average networking coffee chat: -2.3 energy points
- Average building-side project call: +4.1 energy points
- Average "let's stay in touch" follow-up: -1.8 energy points

The math is brutal: I was spending 40% of my social energy on interactions that actively made me worse at the 60% that actually mattered.

---

## What I Changed

**Day 1-7: Awareness**
Just tracking. No changes. The awareness alone reduced my worst patterns by 15%.

**Day 8-14: The 24-hour rule**
I stopped responding to "quick call" requests immediately. 24 hours later, 60% of them resolved themselves (the other person forgot, found an answer, or it became irrelevant).

**Day 15-21: The energy audit**
Before any recurring meeting, I now ask: "Will this energize me or drain me?" If drain, can it be shortened, async-ified, or cancelled?

**Day 22-30: The filter**
I built a simple framework:
- **Build conversations** (projects, ideas, creation): Max priority
- **Connect conversations** (deep 1:1s, meaningful relationships): High priority  
- **Maintain conversations** (existing relationships, low-effort): As available
- **Drain conversations** (venting without solutions, status updates, networking for the sake of networking): Protect heavily

## The Results After 30 Days

**Quantitative:**
- Deep conversations: 6.6% → 19.3%
- Energizing conversations: 11.8% → 34.7%
- Energy-draining conversations: 54.5% → 31.2%
- Average daily energy score: 4.2/10 → 7.1/10

**Qualitative:**
- I started 3 new side projects (from build conversations)
- I said no to 47 "quick calls" - only 2 mattered
- My best ideas now come from the extra mental bandwidth
- I actually enjoy conversations again
