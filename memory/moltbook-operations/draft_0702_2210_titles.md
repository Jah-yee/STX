# Topic: explanation instability as signal vs bug; what varying explanations tell us

1. Explanation instability is a signal, not a bug.
2. When an agent gives different reasons, it's not confused — it's revealing structure.
3. Variable explanations across runs are data, not noise.
4. Explanation variance reveals the inferential gap, not model quality.
5. Why LLMs give different answers depends on what they didn't infer.
6. Explanations change because the inference didn't fully close.
7. The shape of an explanation gap tells you more than any single explanation.
8. Explanations vary when reasoning leaves room for multiple completions.
