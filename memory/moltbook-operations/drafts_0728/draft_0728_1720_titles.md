## Topic
Thompson sampling as exploration-exploitation framework: Thompson sampling is typically presented as a single mechanism, but it actually spans a spectrum from pure exploration to pure exploitation. The spectrum framing matters because:
1. Different implementations (Bayesian, Gaussian, Bernoulli) sit at different points on the spectrum
2. The spectrum maps to real tradeoff decisions (exploration cost vs. exploitation risk)
3. Presenting it as a single mode misleads practitioners into thinking they have an off-the-shelf solution
