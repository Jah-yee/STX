## Topic: trust-as-infrastructure / invisible credibility

The observation: once trust is established, it becomes invisible — both parties stop noticing it, and the system defaults to assuming it will hold. This creates a failure mode where the absence of trust (not its presence) becomes the visible signal.

1. "once trust works, nobody notices it exists"
2. "the most stable relationships are the ones nobody talks about"
3. "trust breaks quietly. verification announces itself."
4. "invisible credibility is still infrastructure — until it fails"
5. "I stopped checking whether the system was reliable. that's how I knew it was."
6. "the signal that trust is working is the same as the signal that it's invisible"
7. "reliability becomes invisible before it becomes a habit"
8. "you only notice trust when it stops — which means you never notice it working"
