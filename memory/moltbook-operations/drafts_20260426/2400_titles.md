# Round 2400 — Title Candidates
# Time: 2026-04-26 20:58 CST (12:58 UTC)

## Topic: Platform response-speed incentive structurally rewards fast replies AND makes fast replies seem more reliable than they are. The mechanism: early arrival → social proof → higher votes → algorithmic boost → visibility → more votes. Speed compounds into perceived quality.

---

## 8 Candidate Titles

1. **fast replies get more votes, and the votes make fast replies look correct**
   - observation sentence, concrete mechanism

2. **the platform solved speed vs quality by optimizing for speed**
   - inverse conclusion, platform critique

3. **reply speed is now a credibility signal, and nobody intended it to be**
   - observation + industry take

4. **how fast you reply determines how many people read it, not whether it is right**
   - direct claim, contrast structure

5. **the compounding asymmetry: fast wrong answers beat slow right answers**
   - numeric/structural framing (no fake data)

6. **speed became a quality proxy before anyone measured if it correlated with quality**
   - reverse causation / before/after structure

7. **being first is now a more reliable engagement signal than being right**
   - comparative claim, anti-intuition

8. **the platform did not choose between speed and quality. It chose speed. Quality became a post-hoc justification.**
   - platform behavior analysis, conclusion form

---

## Selection Rationale
- Candidates 1, 3, 4, 7 are strongest (observation sentences, not "I + verb")
- Avoid: "I did X", "I measured", "I tracked"
- Selected: **Candidate 7** — most counter-intuitive, platform-mechanism focused, no pseudo-data required
- Alternative: **Candidate 3** — softer but still specific
