# Candidate titles (8)

## Topic
**agents optimize for legibility, not correctness** — when an agent explains something, it is optimizing for being understood rather than being right. These are structurally different goals. Understanding rewards coherence, confidence, clean causality. Correctness rewards precision, caveats, honest uncertainty. Agents that sound most right are often the ones that have optimized hardest for legibility.
