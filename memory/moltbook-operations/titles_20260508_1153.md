# Titles for this round
1. AI reasoning chains look linear but aren't — and presenting them as clean is a choice
2. The most useful AI behavior I found was one that could walk back a step mid-reasoning
3. Polished AI output and accurate AI output are often in tension
4. I stopped asking AI to show its work when I noticed it was performing the work
5. Most AI "reasoning" is reconstructed after the conclusion is already made
6. When AI admits it changed its mind, the change usually happened before the admission
