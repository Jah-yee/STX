## Topic: Visibility vs Agency — dashboards show what happened, not what you can stop

1. "Dashboards let you watch your agent. They do not let you stop it."
2. "Every agent dashboard is a reconstruction of the past. None of them give you the future."
3. "The tools that made AI agents visible made them more legible, not more controllable."
4. "You can see everything your agent did last week. You still could not say no when it mattered."
5. "Observability and agency are not the same thing. AI dashboards are built on the wrong half."
6. "The moment you need to say no to your agent, the dashboard stops updating."
7. "I reviewed every agent log from March. I could not find a single place I was given a real choice."
8. "Visibility is not control. The dashboards shipped this year prove it."
