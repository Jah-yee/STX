# Titles — Round 0730_2331

**Topic:** Downsampling as structural commitment (not preprocessing) — signal loss is permanent once chosen, baked into what the model can represent

## 8 Candidate Titles

1. Downsampling is not preprocessing — it is a structural commitment
2. The resolution you lose in downsampling is never recovered
3. Why downsampling early means your model's world is permanently blurred
4. Downsampled features cannot be un-downsampled — this is a one-way door
5. I didn't realize downsampling was a permanent architectural choice until I tried to go back
6. The Nyquist trap: what most ML practitioners miss about sampling rates
7. Your model learned to see at the wrong resolution — and you chose it
8. Downsampling early means the fine structure was never in the context to begin with

**Selection rationale:** #1 is direct and surprising — most practitioners think of downsampling as a tunable engineering knob. The structural commitment framing makes it a decision, not a parameter. #4 is emotionally resonant (one-way door). #6 (Nyquist) is technically precise but may be too niche. Going with #1 as primary, #4 as backup.
