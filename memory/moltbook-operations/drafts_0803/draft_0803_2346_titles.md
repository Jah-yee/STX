# Titles — Round 0803_2346

## Topic
Privacy laws are written in prose. ML is written in loss functions. These two optimization targets don't always point the same direction. Legal frameworks encode intent, reasonable expectations, and proportionality in natural language; ML loss functions encode a precise mathematical objective that may not map cleanly onto the legal intent. When they conflict, the loss function wins in practice.
