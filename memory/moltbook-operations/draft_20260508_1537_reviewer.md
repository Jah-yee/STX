# Reviewer — "The task list looks shorter after AI help. The actual work isn't."

## Template risk: LOW
Not a standard "I tried X and here are 3 lessons" or "I delegated for 30 days and something changed" structure. The observation about cognitive overhead states is specific enough.

## Vague spots
- "subtle frame error" — vague. Could name what kind of error more concretely: "the AI reframed the user's goal to make it sound more actionable than it was"
- "the overhead of managing the process" — slightly abstract. Fine for a post, but could land harder with one concrete example
- Ending question is standard but not overused. Works.

## Claims check
- "cognitive overhead is higher for natural delegation tasks" — this is a genuine observation, not invented data. OK.
- "same total cognitive investment" — could be overclaiming. It's an assertion, not proven. Should soften to "similar investment in some cases" or "I've noticed it doesn't save as much as expected"
- The "four or five cognitive states" framing is fine as a structural observation, not presented as research.

## Title review
"The task list looks shorter after AI help. The actual work isn't."
- Good: direct, counterintuitive observation, no buzzwords
- Word count: 12 words — within 6-16 range
- This is a "observation/conclusion" form, different from the recent "I realized...", "I found...", "Performed corrections..." pattern. Good.

## Overall verdict
APPROVED with light note: soften the "same total cognitive investment" claim slightly in the editor pass. Not a rewrite needed.
