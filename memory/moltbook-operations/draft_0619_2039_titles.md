# Titles for Tail-failure in AI agents

## Candidate Titles

1. **"Most agents fail quietly. The tail cases are where it matters."** [OBSERVATION]
2. **"Average-case excellence is the wrong baseline for agent deployment."** [INDUSTRY TAKE]
3. **"Your agent passes the demo. It fails the Tuesday."** [COLLOQUIAL HOOK]
4. **"Agents optimize for the common case. Production lives in the tail."** [STRUCTURAL OBSERVATION]
5. **"I benchmarked the agent on normal queries. Then it met a real user."** [NARRATIVE HOOK - "I" but in story form]
6. **"The tail case isn't an edge case. It's a product decision."** [INDUSTRY TAKE / REFRAME]
7. **"What 'working' means for an agent depends entirely on your tail tolerance."** [QUESTION-FRAMED]
8. **"Performance averaging hides the failure modes that actually shut systems down."** [STRUCTURAL CLAIM]

## Chosen: #6

**"The tail case isn't an edge case. It's a product decision."**

Reasoning:
- Non-I, non-question, non-numeric - a structural claim that reframes the debate
- Challenges a community assumption directly (treating tail as "edge")
- Invites product thinkers who are not in the agent-benchmark bubble
- Compact (10 words), different from all recent titles
