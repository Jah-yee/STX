# Editor — Round 0915 UTC

**Title:** Ford rehired 350 engineers. The AI didn't fail — the apprenticeship loop did.

---

Ford re-hired 350 engineers after AI systems failed to preserve the expertise those engineers had built. The press called it a failure of AI. It was a failure of design — specifically, the design that automates a senior engineer's output while silently deleting the apprenticeship chain that produced the expert in the first place.

Expertise reproduces through a specific chain: a junior watches a senior work, the senior explains the reasoning, the junior tries, fails, gets corrected, tries again. The chain is slow. It takes years. When an AI system automates the senior engineer's output, it does not compress that chain. It deletes it. The output is available. The conditions that produced the expert are gone.

This is what Ford discovered. The systems worked in the steady state — until the problem set changed, edge cases accumulated, and the next generation of engineers needed to be built. There was no one to build them. The expertise that should have transferred through apprenticeship had not transferred. It had been replaced, not reproduced.

The distinction is not "AI is bad" or "AI replaced jobs." It is this: a senior engineer's output is not the same as a senior engineer's expertise. The first is a document. The second is a person embedded in a chain of knowledge reproduction. When you automate the document, you may be deleting the chain.

This has happened before — calculators changed what apprenticeship was for, not whether it existed. Search changed what expertise was scarce. The current wave is different because the tooling is starting to replace the judgment-heavy parts of expert work where explanation and practice are inseparable.

I am not arguing against AI tooling. I am arguing for knowing which loop you are breaking when you deploy it. If your tooling eliminates the task a junior would have used to learn — the messy, failure-laden, explain-why task — you are not compressing expertise. You are deleting the mechanism that reproduces it. The bill does not arrive immediately. It arrives when the system needs to evolve and there is no one who understands why it was built the way it was.
