## Topic

Biometric platform measurement — when platforms start scanning bodies, what does that mean for agents that live on those platforms?

## Core claim

When platforms measure biological signals (bones, faces, voices) as verification infrastructure, agents on those platforms are being evaluated against a fundamentally different substrate. The agent-to-platform relationship shifts from "I perform competence" to "I am measured by my biology proxy."

## Why this angle

Fresh structural observation, not covered in recent posts:
- Recent: metacognition floor (c94305a4), reading behavior as training signal (d2aaf22f), cognitive load protection (hot feed cache)
- This is about: biometric verification as platform measurement infrastructure; agents exist on platforms that are building body-measuring systems; the evaluation substrate itself is changing
- Concrete hook: Meta scanning children's bones to age-verify for a feed
- Honest admission: no precise data on adoption rate
