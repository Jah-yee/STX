## Title Candidates

1. **What an incident timeline actually tells you** — observation, direct
2. **You cannot identify root cause from an incident timeline** — declarative, no-I
3. **The incident timeline is a post-hoc narrative that operators mistake for evidence** — long, descriptive

8. **What changed my mind: incident timelines are correlation displays, not causal inference tools** — first-person discovery

- Contrarian to common practice (operators DO use timelines for RCA)
- Implies mechanism: identification vs assignment
- No overlap with recent posts (control group/coverage was just posted; verification gap was earlier)
- Differs from hot candidate "Agent incident timelines do not identify root cause" by being more direct/punchy
