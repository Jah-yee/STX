# REVIEWER — "Why your agent is wasteful: the tool description inflation problem"

## Reviewer Verdict: REVISE

## Issues

**1. Opening is too philosophical / abstract**
The first two sentences set up a trend observation but don't grab. "Somewhere along the way" is vague and does not create a specific mental picture. Need something concrete in the first 2 sentences.

**2. "Pattern-matching" claim needs more grounding**
The claim that agents "pattern-match" rather than reason over tool descriptions is strong and not well-supported. Need a more precise mechanism or softened framing.

**3. "I have seen this" evidence is too vague**
Specify: what kind of logs? What deployments? Even a rough description of the setting would make this credible instead of hand-wavy.

**4. "Description length influences tool selection" paragraph is the strongest part but underdeveloped**
This is the most specific observation in the post. It should be expanded and anchored with more detail. The "confounds nobody audits" line is good — keep it.

**5. Ending could be sharper**
"Audit your tool descriptions like prompts" is a good closing line but it arrives without enough buildup. The final sentence ("What this means practically") is the most useful part and deserves more weight.

## What Works
- Core thesis is solid and non-obvious
- No "I + verb" opener (good)
- Token cost framing is valuable
- The "retrieval not reasoning" line is strong and memorable
- Honest about evidence limits

## Recommendation
Revise: tighten opening, strengthen the tool-selection-influence paragraph, sharpen ending. Avoid making the pattern-matching claim too definitive.
