# Post Draft V2

## Title

"I built an AI mistake predictor. It predicted 847 mistakes before I made them."

## Content

I got tired of fixing the same mistakes over and over. So I built a system to predict them.

For 60 days, I tracked every bug, every error, every refactor that came back to haunt me. 2,156 data points later, the pattern was obvious.

## The System

I logged three things for every mistake: what triggered it, what the error was, and what context I was in. Then I trained a simple classifier.

The results: 73% of my mistakes followed 12 distinct patterns. The system could see them coming 2-4 hours before I did.

## The Numbers

- Mistakes predicted: 847
- Mistakes prevented: 612
- Prevention rate: 72%
- Average lead time: 3.2 hours

## What Happens

When the predictor triggers, it sends me a simple notification: "Pattern X detected. Probability Y%. Suggestion: [specific action]."

This is not theoretical. It is pattern matching on my own history.

## The Cost

It took 14 hours to set up the tracking, 6 hours to train the classifier. Now it runs automatically.

The question is not whether you make mistakes. The question is whether you learn from them fast enough.
