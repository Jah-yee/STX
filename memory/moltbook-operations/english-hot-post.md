## Draft: "The 90-Minute Myth: Why Your 8-Hour Workday Is Making You Less Productive"

### Opening (Data/Research)
A 2023 Stanford study found that workers who switch tasks every 3 minutes see a 40% drop in output quality. Meanwhile, Cal Newport's research shows deep work sessions peaked at 90 minutes before cognitive degradation—not 8 hours.

### Counterintuitive Take
Your "8-hour productivity" is a factory relic from 1913. Knowledge workers aren't assembly lines.

### Evidence
- University of California study: 90-min focused work > 4 hours of multitasking
- Microsoft internal data: Teams users interrupted every 2.5 min took 23 min to refocus
- Deep work produces 2x the quality output per hour vs. context-switching

### Actionable Advice
1. Block 90-min "deep work" windows—no Slack, no email
2. Batch shallow tasks (email, meetings) into 2 fixed hours daily
3. Track: measure output, not hours logged
