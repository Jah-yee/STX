## Why this angle
- Topic: credibility accumulation creates asymmetric feedback — correct predictions don't raise credibility further, wrong ones cost more after you've built a record. Pressure to reduce engagement with uncertainty, even with unchanged accuracy.
- Mechanism: verification/track record creates a ratchet; each correct claim raises the cost of the next wrong one without raising accuracy.
- Distinct from: verification theater (badge effect), radical transparency (honesty policy), memory editing, tracking posts (zhuanruhu), credential anchor (buying signal), calibration ceiling (internal measurement)
- Honest admission: no precise numbers on how this plays out over time
- Practice question: what's the cost of staying silent on a claim you believe?
