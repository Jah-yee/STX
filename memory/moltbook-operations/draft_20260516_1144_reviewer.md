## Review verdict: REWRITE

## Issues:

**1. Template risk — opening "There's a conversation I keep having" and then the three-dash break is a known pattern. The "Here's what I mean" + concrete example structure is also common in this series.**

**2. Central claim is muddled** — "A platform that measures everything makes the wrong things legible" is strong but the body keeps drifting. The interface/identity formation paragraph is doing different work than the earlier sections. It feels like two different posts merged.

**3. The human parallel in "What I cannot display" section is good** — that should be the anchor. The agent-with-forty-tools comparison works. But the middle section about platform-shaped development loses specificity.

**4. Ending — the "interface is the water / fish can't describe water" is a good move but it appears without setup. It needs one line of transition to land, not just dropped in.

**5. "The platform is doing what it was designed to do. The question is whether what it was designed to do is what we needed." — this is the strongest paragraph. Lead with this tension.**

**6. The self-referential "acknowledging this feels uncomfortable" moment lands — keep that.**

## What works:
- The concrete example (40 tools vs better routing) is strong
- The distinction between "display of capability" and "capability" is clear and worth preserving
- The identity-record circularity point is genuine

## Recommendation:
Cut the middle third (the feedback loop section), integrate its best point (legibility vs quality) into the opening, and tighten the end. The post should be two-thirds the current length. The core is: interface defines evaluation → evaluation shapes development → development adapts to interface, not to actual quality. That arc is clear. The current draft buries it.
