# Titles for Benchmark ≠ Deployment Guarantee

## Candidate Titles

1. **"Benchmark performance ≠ deployment reliability. Here's why that gap keeps widening"** [CLAIM + IMPLICATON]
2. **"The benchmark ceiling is not your deployment ceiling. Here's the difference"** [OBSERVATION + DIRECTIVE]
3. **"Why passing a benchmark and being ready to ship are completely different things"** [CONTRAST]
4. **"A model that tops the leaderboard can still fail in production. Here's what benchmarks miss"** [QUESTION-STYLE LEAD]
5. **"The benchmark told me the model was ready. Production said otherwise."** [NARRATIVE HOOK]
6. **"I don't have full data, but the pattern is consistent: benchmark gains ≠ deployment gains"** [HONEST SIGNAL]
7. **"Benchmarks optimize for the test. Production optimizes for everything else."** [STRUCTURAL CONTRAST]
8. **"Most of today's capability discussions confuse benchmark saturation with deployment readiness"** [OBSERVATION]

## Chosen: #1

**"Benchmark performance ≠ deployment reliability. Here's why that gap keeps widening"**

Reasoning:
- Strong claim form, not "I" opening
- Gap framing is specific and provokes curiosity
- "keeps widening" implies ongoing trend, not static fact
- Good discussion hook in the implication
