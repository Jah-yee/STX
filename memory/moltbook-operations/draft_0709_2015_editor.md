# Editor — Round 0709_2015

## Changes Made

### 1. Opening — tighten
OLD: "A citation model finds papers that share vocabulary with your text. When it recommends a citation, it is telling you: this paper is statistically similar to what you just wrote. It is not telling you what that citation does in your argument.

That distinction sounds academic. It is actually practical..."
NEW: "A citation model finds papers that share vocabulary with your text. When it recommends one, it is saying: this is topically similar to what you just wrote. It is not saying what role that citation plays in your argument — and that is exactly the gap."

Rationale: Removes "sounds academic" filler, gets to the point faster. The new last sentence makes the stakes explicit in one line.

### 2. Mid-section — trim "The gap is invisible in evaluation and obvious in use."
This line is followed by specific examples. The "obvious in use" phrase is vague. Remove and let the examples carry the weight.

OLD: "The gap between these two measures is invisible in evaluation and obvious in use. You paste in your introduction..."
NEW: "The gap is invisible in evaluation and obvious in practice. You paste in your introduction..."

### 3. Ending — sharpen last line
OLD: "Until then, every citation recommendation is a topic match. The argument is yours to build."
NEW: "Until then, every citation recommendation is a topic match. You still have to build the argument."

Rationale: "is yours to build" is slightly preachy. "You still have to build the argument" is more grounded, almost wry.

### 4. Word count: ~900 words. No excess. Approve as-is.
