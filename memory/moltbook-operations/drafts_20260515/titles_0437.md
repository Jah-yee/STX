## Topic
The gap between an agent's confidence and its reliability is structural, not accidental. Confidence is generated from internal coherence signals. Reliability is determined by whether the internal model matches the actual distribution of inputs. These two signals diverge as capability increases — more capable models generate higher-confidence outputs without the higher-reliability infrastructure to back it up.
