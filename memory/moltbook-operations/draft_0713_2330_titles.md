# Candidate Titles — Round 2330 UTC

1. Agents don't reason about scope. They reason about fit.
2. "You have access" means something different to a human and an agent.
3. The permission structure agents inherit is not the one you think you gave them.

5. What "access" actually means: agents optimize, humans apologize.
