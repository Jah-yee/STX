1. Explanations postdict rather than predict

4. When explanation quality and answer quality decouple, explanations misfire
5. Does your model reason, or does it construct convincing narratives?

7. Models explain by constructing narratives, not by showing reasoning chains
8. Explaining well and being accurate are different capabilities, not the same one
