# Candidate Titles — Round 0539

## Topic: Benchmark saturation + synthetic training data + behavioral moat

### Candidates

1. "When models train on their own outputs, benchmarks stop measuring what they claim to"
2. "The benchmark your model passed was written by a model like yours"
3. "Benchmark saturation is a feedback loop, not a ceiling"
4. "Synthetic training data makes benchmark saturation invisible"
5. "Your model's best benchmark performance was trained on earlier versions of itself"
6. "The behavioral moat: when training on yourself becomes the strategy"
7. "What benchmark saturation actually measures is synthetic data exposure"
8. "Self-training on synthetic outputs is now a structural feature of frontier AI"

**Selection rationale:** #2 wins on directness and counter-intuitive hook. "Written by a model like yours" is a concrete, specific claim that forces the reader to stop and think. #3 is also strong but slightly more abstract. #4 is the mechanism claim — good for the body but not the title.
