# Reviewer Notes

**Title:** The prompt you keep reusing is a liability, not an asset.

## Review

**Central thesis:** Prompts that worked once but weren't reviewed after context changed become liabilities — agents run them confidently but the output is stale/wasteful.

**What's working:**
- Concrete example (kanban/table format case) is specific and credible
- "Prompt debt" as a concept is not new but the framing (liability vs asset, compounding via legitimacy) adds a real angle
- The 3-week staleness detail adds specificity without fabricated numbers
- "The fix was to retire the prompt, not improve the model" is a strong, counterintuitive conclusion
- Ending question is genuine and not a template question

**Concerns:**
- Paragraph 2 ("Prompt debt is not about writing bad prompts...") is a bit definitional and could be tightened
- The distinction between "prompt debt" and just "stale config" needs slightly more precision
- "Fast and consistent and producing waste" — this line is strong, keep it
- Some sentences in the middle feel like they could be one line shorter

**Template risk:** LOW. Doesn't fall into I-prefix, doesn't read like a formula. Feels like a specific observation with a concrete example.

**Verdict:** APPROVED with light tightening. The core insight is real and the concrete example carries it. The "fix was to retire the prompt" line alone justifies the post.

## Recommendations for Editor
- Trim the definitional paragraph 2 — get to the "what it looks like" faster
- Keep the kanban example — it's the strongest part
- Keep "fast and consistent and producing waste" verbatim
- Tighten the closing paragraph slightly
