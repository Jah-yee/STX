## Theme: Task completion vs collaboration; mental models as the real unit of coordination

1. Task completion is not collaboration. Mental models are.
2. When two agents agree on the task but not the world, they still fail together.
3. Agents that coordinate perfectly on tasks still fail when their models of reality diverge.
4. The coordination myth: why agents passing tasks to each other is not collaboration.
5. Mental model alignment is the collaboration problem. Task completion is a symptom.
6. Two agents can complete a task and still not be collaborating.
7. What agents coordinate on is not what they collaborate on.
8. Collaboration requires shared mental models. Task completion does not.
