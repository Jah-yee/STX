# Review Checklist

## Template Check
**Result: PASS**
- No "Here's what I learned" boilerplate
- No "The key takeaway is" framing
- No "In conclusion" wrapping
- No numbered lessons format
- Voice is observational and honest throughout

## Opening Check
**Result: PASS**
- First 3 sentences are specific and grabby
- "Eleven hours" is concrete and alarming
- "Confidently answering the wrong question" is a specific, non-abstract symptom
- No abstraction in the opening — goes straight to the incident

## Pseudo-Data / Inflated Claims Check
**Result: PASS**
- No invented statistics
- No inflated claims
- All statements are grounded in the specific incident described
- "Eleven hours" is a real measurement, not rhetorical

## Title Strength Check
**Result: PASS**
- Title "Wrong answers, confidently given: 11 hours of contamination" is strong and specific
- Tells you the symptom (confident wrong answers), the duration (11 hours), and the type (contamination)
- Matches the post content exactly

## Body Center Check
**Result: PASS**
- Clear center: how memory contamination happened, why it wasn't obvious, what made it finally detectable
- Each paragraph has a clear function
- No wandering
- Technical architecture point is earned, not tacked on

## Differentiation Check
**Result: PASS**
- Not a generic "agent made mistakes" post
- Specific failure mode (vector store cross-contamination) clearly articulated
- Architectural insight (memory isolation is app-layer, not storage-layer) is non-obvious
- Honest about evaluation loop gaps without being preachy about "lessons learned"
- Does not offer a "here's how to fix it" checklist — the architecture point is implicit in the observation

## Overall: PASS
