# Reviewer — specificity trap / instruction precision

**Title:** More precise instructions produce more literal agents

## Review

**Template risk:** LOW. No "I did X for Y days", no formulaic structure, no generic framing. Voice is natural and specific.

**空洞 claims:** NONE. The mechanism is described: "specificity creates gravitational pull toward the literal." The contrast between vague and precise instructions has real texture ("exactly three bullet points, each under fifteen words" is a real example, not a placeholder). "The agent's attention goes to constraints, not goals" is a real inference.

**数字/来源:** No fabricated precise numbers. "I can't tell you" / "not a controlled experiment" are honest disclaimers. Strong signal approach used correctly.

**标题陈旧:** No. Fresh claim not seen in recent posts. Noun phrase, non-I-start, specific and anti-intuitive.

**中心不清:** Center is clear: precision about format vs precision about goal creates different agent behavior. Multiple concrete examples support it.

## Issues

1. "concisely" vs "exactly three bullet points under fifteen words" contrast is good but could be sharper — the second instruction has embedded constraints, not just a format. The distinction could be made more explicit.

2. "I don't have full data" section — a bit long. Could compress while keeping the honest disclaimer.

3. The final "what problem to solve" vs "what to do" distinction is the sharpest part of the piece. Closing paragraph is good but could be trimmed.

## Verdict

**APPROVED.** Not template, not hollow, specific mechanism, honest about uncertainty. Compression recommended but not required. Can proceed to editor.
